title: So, I Made A Blog
date: 2026-08-15
category: Writing, Blog
short: I guess the millions of side projects I already have weren't enough; I needed another one.
---
I've always enjoyed writing. I've always enjoyed putting my thoughts and ideas on paper. My phone's Notes app proves it.

For a while, I focused my writing on weekly LinkedIn posts. Some posts got a little famous in terms of likes and views, but what I really enjoyed was how it sparked incredible conversations in the comments, and how much I learned just from reading other people's opinions on a certain topic. Unfortunately, work got in the way, and I stopped doing these weekly posts.

My entire career is built on communities and sharing knowledge. Take the podcast, for example, or when I joined a Brazilian ONG as a free mentor. All of these experiences, and more, led me to where I'm at today. The networking that I've done and the people that I've met have all led me to where I'm at.

I haven't done that in a while. Worse, I don't spend time sharing knowledge with the community anymore. Yes, I have way too many hobbies and at least 5 unfinished side projects, but I really wanted to take this off the paper and go back to that era of teaching and learning.

The reason is that I realized I don't spend time thinking about what I do, how I do it, or the new stuff I'm learning. That might be normal for most people, but I realized that for me, that means I can't explain that to other people, and worse, I can't teach other people how I do things.

As a Team Lead, I do many things throughout the day without really stopping to think about them. I realized this while training and guiding a newly promoted Team Lead at work. I can't explain to them how I organize my team, my priorities, and my projects. I simply do. It's almost subconscious.

This blog will help me learn and process what I learn, how I do things. Hopefully this will help me improve at teaching people my way of doing things, but also instigate me to think about these things and maybe learn better ways of doing them.

I could've done that on a notepad or in a diary, but I wanted to make it public. Let's solve two problems with a single line change. The blog will help me understand and process my work and experiences, while also getting me slowly back into the community.

Let's see how this goes. Hopefully it doesn't become another forgotten side project. :)
